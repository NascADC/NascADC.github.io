  1. Fundamentals on Image Processing [ Lesson-1 ] [ List-1 ]

    🎓 Image processing is a multidisciplinary field that involves the development and application of mathematical and computational techniques for analyzing, enhancing, and extracting information from digital images. It plays a vital role in numerous domains such as medical imaging, remote sensing, industrial inspection, video surveillance, autonomous navigation, and digital photography.

  2. Representations/Transforms of Images [ Lesson-1 ] [ List-(atual) ]

    🎓 In image processing, representations and transformations are mathematical frameworks that allow us to analyze, edit and compress images by highlighting certain properties such as frequency content, spatial relationships or geometric structure. The choice of a suitable representation is crucial for tasks such as enhancement, segmentation, compression, denoising and feature extraction.

  3. Analysis of Variance in Statistical Image Processing [ Lesson-1 ] [ List-1 ]

    🎓 In statistical image processing, analysis of variance (ANOVA) is a powerful method for evaluating differences in intensity or texture between different regions of an image, especially in segmentation, classification and recognition tasks. ANOVA evaluates whether the observed differences between image regions are statistically significant and provides a formal decision making framework for spatial analysis.

  4. Spatial ARMA Processes and Random Fields [ Lesson-1 ] [ List-1 ]

    🎓 Spatial data analysis involves the study of data indexed by spatial coordinates, often defined over a two-dimensional grid or continuous domain. In this context, random fields and spatial autoregressive moving average (ARMA) processes play a fundamental role in modeling spatial dependencies and structures.
